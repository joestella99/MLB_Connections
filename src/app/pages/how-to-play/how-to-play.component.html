How to Play

Objective

Find four groups of four MLB-related items that share a common connection.

Rules

  • Select four items and tap Submit to check if they belong to the same group.
  • Find all four groups without making 4 mistakes to win!
  • Each puzzle has exactly one solution — there's only one correct grouping.

Difficulty

Categories are color-coded by difficulty:

Yellow — Straightforward
Green — Moderate
Blue — Tricky
Purple — Devious

Tips

  • Watch out for items that could fit in multiple categories — the purple group is designed to deceive!
  • Use Shuffle to rearrange the board if you're stuck.
  • If you're one away, you'll get a hint — use it wisely!
